# TUFF BEATZ V3.4 — Approved Player Specification

**Project:** TUFF BEATZ  
**Producer identity:** Emmanuel Tuffet  
**Brand line:** SOUND. PURPOSE. LEGACY.  
**Status:** Approved HTML design baseline

## Approved player behavior

The music player is collapsed by default and uses the simple V2.2-style gold circular launcher at the bottom-left of the page.

When the launcher is clicked, the premium V3 player expands across the bottom of the browser. The expanded player includes current track information, previous/play-next controls, waveform-style seek/progress, elapsed/duration display, volume control, queue access, and a close/collapse control.

When the visitor scrolls up or down, the expanded player collapses back to the simple circular launcher. Playback must continue while the interface is collapsed.

Clicking a release card opens the expanded player and starts that track.

## Now Playing experience

Clicking the current-track artwork from the expanded player opens the immersive full-screen Now Playing experience. It includes large artwork, track title, artist, genre, BPM, key, producer identification, waveform presentation, play/pause, queue access, and streaming platform links.

## Queue / Full Playlist

The V3 side-panel queue was rejected. The approved queue uses the immersive V2.2-style full-width playlist drawer that rises from the bottom above the persistent player.

Queue header:

- NOW PLAYING / TUFF BEATZ
- Music Player
- SOUND. PURPOSE. LEGACY.

Queue brand section:

- Actual TUFF BEATZ logo — do not use the temporary `TB` text placeholder
- THE PRODUCER IDENTITY OF EMMANUEL TUFFET
- TUFF BEATZ
- IMMERSIVE RELEASES · FULL PLAYLIST · PREMIUM PRODUCER EXPERIENCE

The queue displays track number, artwork, title, artist, genre and play control. The active track receives a highlighted black/gold treatment.

## Visual direction

Maintain the TUFF BEATZ luxury black and metallic-gold identity. Do not replace it with Kentha's purple/neon visual style. Kentha was used only as interaction inspiration; proprietary Kentha code/assets are not part of this implementation.
